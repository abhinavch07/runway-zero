Where to start

Each path is three lessons in a row. Start with the first one and the lesson list shows you the rest.

How a lesson works

  1. Read the step

    The guide on the left says what to do and why. The panel you need opens by itself.

  2. Find the yellow highlight

    The switch or button to use pulses yellow. The step ticks off only when the aircraft is really set that way.

  3. Take the quiz

    Five questions at the end, with the reason after each answer. Get four right to pass.

Also inside

  • Checklists for every aircraft that check the real switch positions. Press C in any lesson.
  • Spoken ATC. Hear the ATIS and your clearance, then pick the right read-back.
  • Cockpit sound. APU and engine starts, pumps, packs and alerts, made in the browser.
  • A real flight model underneath: JSBSim, the open source flight dynamics engine.
  • Progress saved in your browser, with a tick for each lesson and quiz you pass.
